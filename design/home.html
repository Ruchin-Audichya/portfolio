Yatra Sync2026web app

Share one cab. Pay only your part.

Going to the office, station or airport? Yatra Sync finds people heading the same way before you book an Ola or Uber, groups you by departure time, detour, waiting time and seats, then splits the fare by each rider's distance, exact to the paisa.

Matchedshortest paths on a road graph
Splitby distance and seats, to the paisa
Never morethan your solo fare
